# Balaji Textiles

A traditional, elegant, and trustworthy business website for **Balaji Textiles**, a local textile and readymade clothing store in Saravanampatti, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

## Overview

This is a business showcase and product catalogue website (not an e-commerce platform). It introduces the textile shop, showcases clothing collections, and makes it easy for customers to contact or visit the store.

No online payments, shopping carts, or checkout functionality are included.

## Design Direction

- Classic South Indian textile-store aesthetic
- Cream/ivory background with deep maroon, dark red, and subtle gold accents
- Traditional Indian decorative patterns used sparingly
- Elegant serif headings with readable body text
- Fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile

## Sections

- Navigation bar with logo, links, and "Visit Our Store" button
- Hero section with CTAs (Explore Collections, Get Directions)
- About Us
- Collections: Men's Wear, Women's Wear, Kids' Wear, Fabrics & Dress Materials
- Why Choose Us
- Shop Gallery
- Contact & Location (click-to-call, WhatsApp, Google Maps, hours)
- Footer
