# Balaji Textiles

A classic, traditional small-business website for **Balaji Textiles**, a textile and readymade clothing store in Saravanampatti, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

Designed in a classic 2018–2020 business website style — simple, straightforward layout, traditional horizontal navigation bar, maroon/dark red and subtle gold colour scheme, white/light cream background, rectangular sections, and normal rectangular buttons.

## Overview

This is an informational business website (NOT an e-commerce platform). No shopping cart, login, online payment, checkout, or customer dashboard.

## Sections

- Header — business name/logo and contact number
- Navigation — Home | About Us | Products | Gallery | Contact Us (maroon bar, white text)
- Home banner — welcome heading, introduction, "View Products" button
- About Us — two-column layout (shop image left, description right)
- Our Products — Men's Wear, Women's Wear, Kids' Wear, Fabrics & Dress Materials
- Photo Gallery — conventional rectangular image grid
- Contact Us — address, phone, WhatsApp, hours, Google Maps, Call Now / WhatsApp / Get Directions buttons
- Footer — business info, navigation links, contact, copyright
